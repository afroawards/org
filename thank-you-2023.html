Celebrating Excellence: Air Peace CEO, Olubadan, and More Shine at AFRO AWARDS 2023

Dr. Allen Onyema, the visionary behind Air Peace, and His Royal Highness, Oba Dr. Moshood Olalekan-Balogun, the distinguished Olubadan of Ibadan, are among the esteemed nominees for the prestigious Afro Awards 2023 in recognition of their exceptional humanitarian contributions.

The Afro Awards, an annual non-profit event, stands as a beacon of excellence that pays tribute to remarkable individuals and organizations, fostering Afro-centric excellence in diverse domains. These honors were announced in a statement released on Monday by Mr. Taiwo Oduala, the Founder and Executive Producer of the Afro Awards (AFROAWARDS™).

Mr. Oduala, a renowned Film Director based in the United States, expressed, "These awardees, hailing from both Africa and America, have not only excelled in their respective fields but have also profoundly impacted lives and spurred economic transformations in nations."

Individuals like Ifechukwu Onyema of Air Peace Airlines have made indelible marks on countless lives through their unwavering commitment to humanitarian principles, exemplified by the free evacuation initiatives. Similarly, the Olubadan of Ibadan has stood as a paragon of royal leadership and distinction.

Nigeria has proudly produced men and women of unwavering patriotism and influence, not only within its borders but also on the global stage. Monica Swaida, known as Monicazation, a Nigerian-American singer, actress, movie producer, and CEO of an insurance company, also features among the illustrious awardees.

Air Peace Airlines has demonstrated exceptional dedication by conducting challenging evacuations, from Nigerians in South Africa during the Xenophobic crisis to Sudan and China, in addition to facilitating the return of Indians and Chinese nationals to their home countries from Nigeria.

The primary objective of the Afro Awards (AFROAWARDS™) is to spotlight and honor individuals who have exhibited exceptional humanitarian excellence in various fields and endeavors.

This multimedia and film director further revealed that since its inception, the Afro Awards (AFROAWARDS™) has celebrated over 5,000 individuals at the forefront of humanitarianism and philanthropy in both American and American-African communities.

The 2023 Awardees also include esteemed personalities such as the Mayor of Los Angeles, Karen Bass, and the iconic American hip-hop legend, Calvin Cordozar-Broadus Jr., famously known as Snoop Dogg.

Other deserving honorees encompass Africa Magic TV Network (Multichoice Nigeria Limited), Chief Diran Alabi (Agic Energy North America/ TAG The Agic Group Holdings), Ose Oyamendan (Founder/CEO Nollywood in Hollywood), Innocent Ekakitie (Nigerian-American child star actor), Yung Muusik (Recording artiste/Singer/Songwriter/Model/Actor), Danielle Crawley (Casting Director), ISO Kenny (Artiste/Songwriter/Performer), 19 Keys (Community Activist/Global Thought Leader), and Tarek Stevenson, Entertainment Executive/Influencer Oscar Brunch, among others.

AFROAWARDS™ 2023 marks the second edition, following the successful inaugural event last year. Notable awardees from previous editions include Gov. Ademola Adeleke, Davido, and the First Lady of Sierra Leone, Mrs. Fatima Maada, among other distinguished personalities.

Today's event is hosted at the prestigious Directors Guild of America (DGA), located at 7920 Sunset Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90046, U.S., commencing with a two-day Pre-event. The Afro Awards Conference Experience is scheduled for September 14, followed by Afro Awards USA, AFROAWARDS™ Arts & Fashion Show, and Gala Night on September 15.

We extend our heartfelt congratulations to all the distinguished honorees and look forward to an inspiring celebration of excellence and humanitarianism.
