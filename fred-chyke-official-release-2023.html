PRESS RELEASE

On behalf of Oduataj Entertainment Productions USA in Collaboration with Global Raw Talents, we are pleased to offer our full endorsement support for UGWUMADU FREDERICK LONGINUS CHINWIKE, professionally known as Fred Chyke, in support of his application for the UK Global Talent Visa under the Arts and Culture pathway.

As a U.S. based creative company, we have closely followed Fred’s impressive artistic journey within the music industry. His dedication, professionalism, and remarkable talent have earned him deep respect and admiration both within and beyond the AfroFusion genre.

Fred Chyke stands out as a dynamic voice in contemporary African music, seamlessly blending Afrobeats, reggae, R&B, and traditional storytelling into a rich and innovative sound. His ability to merge heritage with modern music makes him a compelling and influential figure whose impact crosses borders and cultures.

Our engagement with Fred's work has revealed an artist whose singles — including Forever, Nnenna, and Feels — have resonated emotionally with wide audiences and received well-earned industry recognition via platforms such as BBC Introducing, curated Spotify playlists, and various international showcases.

His live performances are consistently celebrated for their energy, authenticity, and audience connection.

In addition to his artistic accomplishments, Fred is a cultural advocate. As the founder of Afrobeats Pop-up UK, he’s actively creating opportunities for underrepresented artists to share their voices. His leadership continues to shape and elevate the global Afrocentric music scene.

We are confident that Fred Chyke embodies the qualities of a globally recognized talent. His evolving contributions to the UK’s cultural landscape position him perfectly for continued artistic and industry growth. We fully support his application and encourage the Home Office to acknowledge both his proven achievements and future potential.

For any further information or clarification, please don’t hesitate to reach out.
